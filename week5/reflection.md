# Stage 2 – Reflection 

Copilot noticed that FR-09 only showed booked appointments, not when practitioners were actually free. I missed this because I was so focused on getting bookings working that I forgot the main problem in the case study was not being able to see practitioner availability.

Copilot suggested adding checks for phone numbers and dates of birth, but the client never asked for that. It also called reports a gap, even though I'd left them out on purpose. I marked them as provisional and added them to my open questions, because the case study never says which reports are needed.

After the review I changed NFR-01 from "the name or time is left empty" to "the patient name is empty, or the appointment time is empty", because "name" on its own could have meant anyone's name. I also updated FR-09 so it shows free time slots as well as booked appointments.

Requirements must come from the client and the case study, not from AI, because AI can come up with things that sound reasonable but that nobody actually asked for. Copilot was helpful for finding gaps and unclear wording, but I still had to check every suggestion myself before using it.
